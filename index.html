“EXPLORING THE USE OF ONLINE COLLABORATIVE TOOLS IN ENHANCING THE TEAMWORK SKILLS OF ICT STUDENTS”


This learning portal is just going to be a prototype to propose our research on and will not be a fully functional application.






canva

Launched in 2013, Canva is an online design and visual communication platform with a mission to empower everyone in the world to design anything and publish anywhere.



microsoft teams

Microsoft Teams is the ultimate messaging app for your organization—a workspace for real-time collaboration and communication, meetings, file and app sharing, and even the occasional emoji! All in one place, all in the open, all accessible to everyone.



google classroom

Google Classroom is a free blended learning platform developed by Google for educational institutions that aims to simplify creating, distributing, and grading assignments.



NOTION

Notion is a single space where you can think, write, and plan. Capture thoughts, manage projects, or even run an entire company — and do it exactly the way you want.



ZOOM

Zoom is a video conferencing platform that can be used through a computer desktop or mobile app, and allows users to connect online for video conference meetings, webinars and live chat.



Conclusion


The use of online collaborative tools has proven to be a valuable strategy in enhancing the teamwork skills of ICT students. As the digital landscape continues to evolve, these tools provide an essential platform for students to engage in collaborative tasks, develop critical communication and problem-solving abilities, and experience the dynamics of working in teams in a virtual environment.

Through the integration of collaborative platforms such as Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Notion, Zoom, and others, students are able to simulate real-world scenarios where teamwork and effective collaboration are key to success. These tools allow for seamless interaction, sharing of resources, brainstorming, and project management, all of which are vital skills for ICT professionals.

In conclusion, the use of online collaborative tools significantly enhances the teamwork skills of ICT students, preparing them for the demands of the digital workplace. These tools promote better communication, organization, and efficiency, while providing students with real-world experiences that will be invaluable in their professional careers. The continued integration and evolution of these tools in education will likely further strengthen their ability to work collaboratively in diverse and technologically advanced environments.
